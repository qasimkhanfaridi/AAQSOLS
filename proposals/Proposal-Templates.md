# AAQSOLS Proposal Templates

## Rules — Read This First (Avoid Auto-Rejection)

Platforms like Upwork flag proposals that look copy-pasted or AI-generated. Follow these rules:

### DO
- Mention **one specific thing** from THEIR job post in the first line
- Keep it **short** (150–300 words for Upwork cover letter)
- Include **one real example** from your experience (Zindigi wallet, integrations, etc.)
- Ask **one question** about their project
- Write like you are messaging a colleague, not writing an essay
- Customize every single proposal — change at least 3 sentences

### DON'T
- Start with "Dear Hiring Manager" or "I hope you are doing well"
- Use phrases: "I am the perfect fit", "Look no further", "I have read your requirements carefully"
- List 15 skills in bullet points
- Write more than 300 words for Upwork
- Send the same proposal to every job
- Use words: "leverage", "utilize", "cutting-edge", "passionate", "seamlessly", "robust solution"
- Attach a generic portfolio — mention ONE relevant project instead
