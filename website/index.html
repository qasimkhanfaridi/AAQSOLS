FAQ

Questions from international clients

Everything you need to know before hiring an offshore team in Pakistan.

Where is AAQSOLS based?

We are based in Islamabad, Pakistan with a full office at Gulberg Greens. We serve clients in the US, UK, UAE, Australia and worldwide via remote collaboration.

What is your hourly rate?

Our rates start from $25/hr for dedicated development teams. We offer monthly retainers, fixed-price projects and time-and-material engagements depending on project scope.

Do you work with US and UK clients?

Yes. We have timezone overlap with US Eastern mornings and UK afternoons. We run weekly video calls, share sprint boards and deliver demos on a regular cycle.

What technologies do you specialise in?

ASP.NET Core, C#, React, Next.js, SQL Server, Entity Framework, Azure, REST APIs and microservices. Our 10-person team covers full-stack development end to end.

How do I start a project?

Email us at Info@aaqsols.com with your project details. We reply within one business day with an honest assessment and proposed next steps.

What industries do you serve?

Healthcare & pharma, travel & booking, e-commerce & retail, and enterprise SaaS platforms. All our projects are production-deployed with real end users.
