About AAQSOLS

A Pakistan-based software development company built by engineers who ship production systems.

AAQSOLS Private Limited is a Pakistan-based software development company focused on .NET, microservices, and financial technology. We are a 10-person team in Islamabad — backend developers, full-stack engineers, QA, DevOps, and project management.
